About Generation Model School & College

A learning community built around academic progress, student wellbeing and personal development.

Our School

Generation Model School & College is located in Tehkal Payan, Peshawar. Public education records list the institution as a Higher Secondary school, while examination records show students appearing under the Generation Model School name.

Our Approach

The school promotes the idea that healthy children learn better. Physical activity and a balanced school experience can support students' fitness, confidence and readiness to learn.
